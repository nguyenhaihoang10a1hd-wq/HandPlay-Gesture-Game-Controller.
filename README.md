How to control the game:

Show a gesture to the webcam to press and hold its corresponding key.

Change or remove the gesture to release the key.

Hold the index-finger-up gesture with your right hand to repeatedly tap J.

Press Q in the webcam window to exit the program.

Note: The program uses the camera's mirrored image to detect hand gestures. Keep your hands clearly visible for better recognition.
